# Success Model & Review Cadence

## Domain Rotation (for selecting daily/weekly questions)

- **Skill** — deliberate practice, feedback, learning, expertise, specialization, generalism, skill transfer
- **Career** — career capital, reputation, credentials, specialization, opportunity selection, negotiation, mobility
- **Wealth** — income, ownership, investing, entrepreneurship, leverage, compounding, risk
- **Productivity** — attention, focus, deep work, time management, multitasking, systems, energy
- **Psychology** — motivation, self-control, discipline, personality, confidence, decision-making, resilience
- **Health** — sleep, exercise, nutrition, stress, energy
- **Social Capital** — relationships, network, trust, mentorship, collaboration, reputation
- **Environment** — geography, institutions, culture, socioeconomic conditions, access to opportunity
- **Strategy** — opportunity selection, leverage, positioning, timing, optionality, competition, comparative advantage
- **Luck** — randomness, timing, exposure to opportunity, path dependence, survivorship bias

Rotate across these rather than repeating one domain session after session; note in the log which domain a session covered so `review` can check coverage.

```text

  What currently appears to contribute to success?

Confidence (per major belief):
  Strong evidence / Moderate evidence / Weak evidence / Unknown
  (No numerical scores unless the underlying evidence genuinely supports quantitative scoring.)

  What changed since last month?

Biggest Unknowns:
  What still requires investigation?

Highest-Leverage Experiments:
  Which personal experiments could provide the most useful information next?
```
